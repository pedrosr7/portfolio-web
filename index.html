● Product · Solo-built · CrossFit & HYROX · Live on both stores

Aura: HYROX & WOD Timer

A full-stack training app for CrossFit & HYROX athletes — designed, built and shipped solo.

Aura turns a photo of your box's whiteboard into a workout you can train and log in seconds, tracks every PR and kilo, and shows who else trained it. I own the whole stack end to end — shared logic, the UI on both platforms, the AI, the backend, and even monetization, ASO and legal — shipped on Google Play and the App Store under my own company, Pampa.

  • Architecture — Kotlin Multiplatform: shared MVI, Koin DI, SQLDelight cache, Ktor client.
  • UI — Compose Multiplatform: one UI codebase for Android and iOS.
  • Backend — Ktor + Exposed + PostgreSQL + Flyway, JWT auth.
  • AI vision — reads a photo of the box whiteboard into a structured, loggable workout.
  • AI coach — post-session insights and adaptive weekly planning.
  • The Whiteboard — a social layer showing who at your box trained the same WOD.

Polymarket — Mobile

Shipping iOS and Android in lockstep by writing Swift that transpiles to Kotlin.

I work on Polymarket's mobile app (via The Left Bit), where a single Swift codebase is transpiled to Kotlin with the Skip toolchain and runs natively on both platforms. My focus: keeping Android at pixel-and-behaviour parity with iOS across real-time chat, live trading and event detail — at consumer scale.

  • Skip transpiler — one Swift codebase → native Kotlin/Android.
  • iOS ↔ Android parity — chat, reactions, live trade, event detail, sports.
  • Real-time — live chat and market/position updates.
  • Native surfaces — SwiftUI on iOS, Jetpack Compose on Android.
